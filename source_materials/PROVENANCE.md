# Source provenance

This integrated project was assembled from the user-supplied archives and documents in the source list below. The extracted original source and generated image-pair dataset are included in the project tree; the large prototype ZIP is not duplicated inside the final ZIP because its contents are extracted under `src/` and `results/baseline/`.

- `NOVA-Loc-source.zip`: original generator/baseline/inference source and prior source metrics.
- `NOVA-Loc-prototype.zip`: source plus 30 DRAM and 30 FinFET generated reference/search image pairs, and ground-truth CSVs.
- `NOVA-Loc-unified-dataset-factory.zip`: source manifest generator, artificial-pair generator, split generator, source config and documentation.
- `RESULTS.md`, `experimental_protocol.md`, and `math.md`: incorporated into `results/` / `docs/`.
- The uploaded original `NOVA.ipynb` is preserved at `source_materials/NOVA_original_backup.ipynb`.

The final package does not contain third-party real SEM image archives; the included pairs are synthetic generated examples. The prototype dataset ground-truth coordinates are generator-derived coordinates.
