1. SEMICON India Hackathon 2026 official problem statement — Drift-Sense requirements, generator contract, official test design, submission requirements.
2. ETH Zurich Scientific Center for Optical and Electron Microscopy, “SEM – Imaging with Secondary Electrons” — secondary-electron edge effect and increased edge brightness.
3. Micron (2026), “SEM-based spatial analysis of rough surfaces: Topographic edge effects and their mitigation” — edge-induced overbrightening in SEM imaging.
4. Yang et al. (2026), “On Determination Method for Resolution of Secondary Electron Images in Scanning Electron Microscopy,” Advanced Science — simulated SEM noise, including Poisson and Gaussian components.
5. Sim et al./Frontiers review/application: “Training Deep Neural Networks to Reconstruct Nanoporous Structures From FIB Tomography Images Using Synthetic Training Data” — electron-microscopy noise modeling with Poisson and Gaussian components.
6. Bals et al. (2023), “Artificial Scanning Electron Microscopy Images Created by Generative Adversarial Networks from Simulated Particle Assemblies,” Advanced Intelligent Systems — synthetic SEM image generation and Gaussian noise.
7. Mattoccia, Tombari & Di Stefano (2011), “Efficient template matching for multi-channel images” — zero-mean normalized cross-correlation and exhaustive template matching.
8. Almira, Phelippeau & Martinez-Sanchez (2024), “Fast normalized cross-correlation for template matching with rotations,” Journal of Applied Mathematics and Computing — efficient NCC and rotation-aware matching.
9. Buniatyan et al. (2017), “Deep Learning Improves Template Matching by Normalized Cross Correlation” — learned preprocessing / Siamese features can reduce false matches in NCC-based correspondence.
10. Nature Scientific Reports (2019), “Unraveling structural and compositional information in 3D FinFET electronic devices” — periodicity of FinFET structures and measurable fin pitch.
